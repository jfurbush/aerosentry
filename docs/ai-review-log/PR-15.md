## Checks I ran beyond the findings

The review listed "strict positive integer `engine_id`" as checked but showed no evidence. I tested it against a running server (POST /predict):

- `{"engine_id": 0}` and `-1`: 422, "Input should be greater than or equal to 1".
- `1.5`, `true` and `"1"`: 422, "Input should be a valid integer". Booleans and numeric strings are not coerced.
- `9999`: 404, `{"detail":"Engine 9999 not found"}`. The message echoes only the integer.

Result: the claim holds. No failing input found.

I did not test 503 or 500 by hand. They are covered in tests/integration/test_api.py by `test_missing_model_gives_503_logs_it_and_hides_the_path`, `test_missing_database_gives_503` and `test_model_and_database_that_do_not_match_give_500`. I rely on CI passing for those.
